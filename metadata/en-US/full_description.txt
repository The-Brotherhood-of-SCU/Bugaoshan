Bugaoshan is a free and open-source campus assistant app designed for Sichuan University (SCU) students. It aggregates essential campus services into one convenient platform.

<b>Key features:</b>

• Course schedule management — Import your timetable from the academic affairs office, view daily schedules, and use the home screen widget for quick access
• Grade tracking — View grades, analyze pass rates, and track academic progress
• Exam schedule — Check upcoming exam dates and arrangements
• Physical fitness test records — View your fitness test results
• Classroom availability — Find empty classrooms across campus in real time
• Balance inquiry — Check campus card balance, network fees, dormitory electricity, and air conditioning balances with historical trend analysis
• Academic calendar — View the university calendar and holiday schedules
• Second classroom activities — Browse and register for extracurricular activities
• Training program inquiry — View training plans for different grades and colleges
• Campus network device management — View and manage online devices on your account
• Notice board — Access announcements from the academic affairs office, student affairs department, and more
• Volunteer platform — Query and register for volunteer activities
• Personalization — Customize themes, course table styles, fonts, app icons, and animation duration

Bugaoshan is built with Flutter and is licensed under AGPL-3.0. No proprietary services (Firebase, GMS, etc.) are used. All data is fetched directly from official SCU services — no third-party tracking or analytics.
